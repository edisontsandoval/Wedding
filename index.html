Our Story

How we got here

The short version: Edison talked too much, Mary Lisa laughed anyway, and neither of us has gone home early since.

  1. 2018

    A borrowed umbrella

    We met outside a coffee shop on Hazeldean Road during a downpour. Mary Lisa had an umbrella and Edison did not. He offered to walk her to her car, which made no sense since it was her umbrella. She said yes anyway.

  2. 2019

    The first trip

    Our first real trip was a weekend in Prince Edward County. We got lost twice, ate far too many butter tarts, and found out we can sit in a car for six hours and still have things to say.

  3. 2022

    One set of keys

    We moved into our first place in Kanata. It came with a crooked kitchen drawer and a balcony just big enough for two chairs and a tomato plant that never once produced a tomato.

  4. 2025

    The question

    Edison had a whole plan involving a picnic at sunset. It rained, of course. He proposed in the kitchen instead, under the same umbrella from 2018, which he had kept in the closet for seven years without telling her.

  5. 2027

    See you at Stonefields

    We toured ten venues across Ottawa and the towns around it. The moment we walked up to the old stone farmhouse, we looked at each other and stopped comparing.

The Venue

Stonefields Estate

Stonefields is a 120-acre heritage farm just outside Carleton Place, with an original stone farmhouse, old log barns and long views over the fields. We'll have the whole property to ourselves for the day.

The ceremony is outside in front of the farmhouse. If the weather has other plans, the team can move everything indoors to the Ceremony House, so there's no need to worry about the forecast.

Questions & Answers

Good to know

Why a Monday?

We fell for Stonefields, and a Monday gave us the date we wanted. We know it means a day off work for most of you, so we're telling you now. Thank you for making the time.

Why do you need my reply so early?

Some of our family and friends are flying in from overseas, and they need time for flights, visas and time off. Knowing who is coming by December 1 lets us help them plan and hold rooms nearby. If something changes later, just let us know.

Can I bring a plus-one?

If your invitation includes a guest, their name will be on it and you can add them on the RSVP form. We're keeping the list close to 75, so we can't add extra guests beyond that.

Are kids welcome?

We love your kids. Children named on the invitation are very welcome.

What if it rains?

The ceremony moves indoors to the Ceremony House. Everything else is under cover already.

Is there parking?

We'll confirm parking details closer to the day.

Do you have a registry?

Having you there is plenty. If you'd still like to give something, details will be here soon.

Who do I ask if something comes up?
